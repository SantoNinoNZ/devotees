Daily Petition (Day 3):

"O Señor Santo Niño, who in your divine wisdom came into the world as a humble child, teach us humility and purity of heart. Look with mercy upon the families of our community in New Zealand and across our homeland. Grant peace, protection, and renewed faith to our households."

Supplications:

• For our loved ones who are sick: Hear us, O Señor Santo Niño.

• For strength in our daily work and studies: Hear us, O Señor Santo Niño.

• For the preservation of our Christian devotion: Hear us, O Señor Santo Niño.
