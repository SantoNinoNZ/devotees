Opening Prayer

"O Señor Santo Niño, who in your boundless love became a humble child for our salvation, look with tenderness upon the families of our community in New Zealand and across our beloved homeland. Bless our parents, protect our children, and grant health to our loved ones."

Supplications for Day 3:

• For our overseas workers and immigrants navigating life in NZ: Hear us, O Holy Child Jesus.

• For the sick, elderly, and those awaiting surgery: Hear us, O Holy Child Jesus.

• For peace and purity in all Christian households: Hear us, O Holy Child Jesus.
