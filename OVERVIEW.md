# Santo Niño NZ Devotees App

## A Digital Home for Our Community

The Santo Niño NZ Devotees App is a mobile-friendly application designed to bring our community closer together. Whether you're attending Sinulog, submitting a prayer request, or coordinating dance practice, this app makes it easy to stay connected.

---

## Who Is This App For?

This app is for **everyone** in our Santo Niño devotee community in New Zealand:

- **Devotees & Families** - Stay updated on events, submit prayer requests, and follow along with novena prayers
- **Hermanos & Hermanas** - Receive and respond to prayer and house visit requests
- **Area Coordinators** - Manage community members and events in your area
- **Dance Groups** - Coordinate practice schedules and track attendance
- **Sinulog Organizers** - Post events, announcements, and casting calls
- **Sinulog Queens & Participants** - Showcase achievements and stay connected

---

## What Can You Do With the App?

**Stay Updated on Events**
- View all upcoming events in a calendar
- See event details, location, and times

- Get reminders before events start

- Track the status of your requests

- Schedule home blessings or visits

- Read announcements from coordinators

- View member directory for your area
- Coordinate with other Hermanos

- See all members in your area (Pukekohe, North Shore, etc.)

**Communicate Effectively**
- Send announcements to your area

### For Dance Leaders

**Organize Practice Sessions**
- Create practice schedules

- See who's planning to attend

**Track Attendance**
- Members RSVP before practice
- Take attendance at each session

### For Sinulog Organizers

**Manage Major Events**

## Community Roles Explained

### Devotee
Every community member starts as a Devotee. You can:
- View all public events
- Submit prayer and house visit requests
- Follow novena prayers
- RSVP to events
- Read announcements

### Hermano / Hermana
Our spiritual leaders who serve the community. They can:
- Do everything a Devotee can do
- Receive and respond to prayer requests
- Handle house visit requests in their area
- Post community announcements
- Lead prayer sessions

### Area Coordinator
Leaders who oversee specific geographic areas. They can:
- Do everything a Hermano/Hermana can do
- Manage members in their area
- Assign Hermano/Hermana roles
- Create area-specific events
- Coordinate across their community

### Dance Leader
Coordinates dance groups for Sinulog and other events. They can:
- Create and manage practice schedules
- Track attendance
- Communicate with dance group members
- Organize performance logistics

### Sinulog Queen
Past and present pageant title holders. They have:
- Special profile highlighting their achievements
- Featured spot in the Queens Gallery
- Ability to share their journey

### Super Admin
System administrators who can:
- Manage all users and roles
- Configure system settings
- Handle technical aspects
- Support all community functions

## How Areas Work

The app organizes our community by geographic area:

- **Pukekohe**
- **North Shore**
- **Auckland Central**
- **South Auckland**
- **Wellington**
- **Christchurch**
- *...and more as our community grows*

**Why areas matter:**
- Prayer requests go to Hermanos in YOUR area
- House visits are coordinated locally
- Some events are area-specific
- Coordinators can focus on their community

You'll select your area when you first join, and it can be updated anytime.

## Installing the App

This app works like a regular app on your phone, but you access it through your web browser.

### On Your Phone (iPhone or Android)

1. Open your phone's web browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.)
2. Go to the app website
3. Look for "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
4. Tap to install
5. Find the app icon on your home screen

### On Your Computer

1. Open Chrome or Edge browser
2. Go to the app website
3. Click the install icon in the address bar
4. Click "Install"

**Benefits of installing:**
- Quick access from home screen
- Works even with poor internet connection
- Receive push notifications
- Feels like a native app

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: Do I need to download anything?**
A: No! The app works in your web browser. You can optionally "install" it to your home screen for easier access.

**Q: Is it free?**
A: Yes, the app is completely free for all community members.

**Q: Do I need a smartphone?**
A: The app works on any device with a web browser - smartphones, tablets, and computers.

**Q: Is my information private?**
A: Yes. Your personal details are only visible to coordinators and Hermanos who need them to serve you. Prayer requests can be marked as private.

### Account

**Q: Why do I need a Google account?**
A: Google sign-in is secure and means you don't need to remember another password. Your Google password is never shared with us.

**Q: Can I use multiple accounts?**
A: We recommend using one account to keep all your information in one place.

**Q: What if I don't have a Google account?**
A: You can create a free Google account at accounts.google.com.

### Prayer Requests

**Q: Who sees my prayer request?**
A: By default, Hermanos and Coordinators in your area can see and respond to your request. You can mark it as private so only the assigned responder sees the details.

**Q: How long until someone responds?**
A: Our Hermanos aim to acknowledge requests within 24-48 hours. Urgent requests are prioritized.

**Q: Can I submit a prayer request for someone else?**
A: Yes! You can submit prayer intentions for family members or friends.

### House Visits

**Q: What happens during a house visit?**
A: Hermanos visit your home for blessings, prayer sessions, or spiritual support. You can specify the purpose when requesting.

**Q: How do I prepare for a house visit?**
A: Just make sure someone is home at the scheduled time. The visiting Hermano will bring what's needed for the blessing.

**Q: Can I request a specific Hermano?**
A: You can add a note to your request, and we'll try to accommodate when possible.

### Events

**Q: How do I find events in my area?**
A: The events calendar shows all events. You can filter by area or event type.

**Q: What does RSVP mean?**
A: RSVP lets the organizers know if you're planning to attend. It helps them prepare for the right number of people.

**Q: Can I suggest an event?**
A: Contact your Area Coordinator to suggest events for your community.

### Dance Groups

**Q: How do I join a dance group?**
A: Contact the Dance Leader through the app or speak with your Area Coordinator.

**Q: What if I can't make a practice?**
A: Update your attendance status in the app and add a note. The Dance Leader will see your response.

## Sinulog Events

The app is designed around the annual Sinulog Festival, celebrated every January (same as in Cebu, Philippines).

### Key Sinulog Events Supported

- **Novena Masses** - Nine days of prayer leading to the feast
- **Sinulog Festival** - Main celebration with processions and performances
- **Sinulog Queen Pageant** - Annual pageant with casting calls
- **Street Dancing** - Coordinated through dance group features
- **Community Gatherings** - Celebrations across all areas

### Year-Round Activities

While Sinulog is in January, our community is active year-round:

- Regular prayer sessions
- Monthly novenas
- Community gatherings
- Dance practice and performances
- House blessings
- Special feast days
