# Santo Niño NZ Devotees App - Quick Summary

## What Is It?
A mobile-friendly app for our Santo Niño devotee community in New Zealand. Install it on your phone like an app, but access it through your browser.

---

## Who Can Use It?

| Role | What They Can Do |
|------|------------------|
| **Everyone** | View events, submit prayer requests, follow novenas, RSVP to events |
| **Hermano/Hermana** | Respond to prayer requests, handle house visits, post announcements |
| **Coordinators** | Manage their area (Pukekohe, North Shore, etc.), assign roles |
| **Dance Leaders** | Create practice schedules, track attendance |

---

## Main Features

**Events**
- View all Sinulog and community events
- See what's coming up on the calendar
- RSVP so organizers can prepare

**Prayer Requests**
- Submit your intentions
- Hermanos in your area receive and pray for you
- Track your request status

**House Visits**
- Request home blessings
- Pick your preferred date and time
- Hermanos coordinate the visit

**Novena Prayers**
- Access prayers anytime
- Track your daily progress
- Get reminders to pray

**Announcements**
- Stay informed about community news
- See casting calls for pageants
- Find your area coordinator

**Dance Schedules**
- See practice times and locations
- RSVP to let the leader know you're coming
- Check attendance history

---

## How It Works

1. **Sign in** with your Google account
2. **Set up** your profile and select your area
3. **Explore** events, prayers, and announcements
4. **Engage** by RSVPing, submitting requests, and connecting

---

## Areas Covered
Pukekohe | North Shore | Auckland | Wellington | Christchurch | and more...

Your area determines which Hermanos respond to your requests and which local events you see.

---

## Key Benefits

- **Stay Connected** - Never miss an event or announcement
- **Get Support** - Prayer and house visits are just a tap away
- **Stay Organized** - Coordinators and dance leaders have tools to manage activities
- **Accessible** - Works on any phone, tablet, or computer

---

## Privacy
- Your contact info is only visible to coordinators/Hermanos
- Prayer requests can be private
- We never share your data with outsiders
